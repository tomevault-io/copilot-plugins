# MerchRoute

> Tome by [kyleliu-ai](https://github.com/kyleliu-ai/MerchRoute), graded and kept true across every major AI platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

MerchRoute is a  e-commerce platform for managing product procurement, AI-powered image and video generation and review, pricing, shipping, and automated or manual product listing on Wildberries and Ozon. It integrates PostgreSQL, n8n workflows, and media-generation services while keeping business data and credentials under the user's control.
