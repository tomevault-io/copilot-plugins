# Karoo-KSafe

> Source: [lockevod/Karoo-KSafe](https://github.com/lockevod/Karoo-KSafe). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

 KSafe: free open-source Karoo safety + ride wellness extension. Reactive layer alerts your contacts on crash, missed check-in, speed drop, HR flatline or   sustained high HR. Preventive layer tracks carbs and hydration with FIT export to Strava / Intervals.icu. Includes manual SOS, custom messages, and webhook   actions.
