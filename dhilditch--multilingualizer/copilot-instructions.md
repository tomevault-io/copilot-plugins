# Multilingualizer growth rules

## Mission

Increase qualified organic traffic that leads to a measurable Multilingualizer sale, Multicurrencyalizer sale or Weglot affiliate click/conversion. Traffic without commercial or learning value is not the goal.

## Read order

1. `.docs/mission.md`
2. `.docs/operating-loop.md`
3. the latest `.docs/baseline-audit-*.md`
4. current GSC/GA4 outputs in `data/` when credentials are available
5. `.docs/seo-playbook.md`

## Working rules

- Use British spelling, no em dashes and no marketing hype.
- Anything published as Dave follows the `dave-voice` skill, article or KB register as appropriate.
- Use one primary search intent per canonical URL. Check existing content and GSC before proposing a new URL.
- Prefer refreshing, consolidating and internally linking a page that already has signals over publishing a competing page.
- Every brief states the target query, search intent, evidence sources, product fit, CTA, competing internal URLs and success metric.
- Never invent product behaviour, platform compatibility, legal requirements, prices, customer experience or performance numbers. Verify time-sensitive claims immediately before publication.
- Create WordPress drafts first. Do not publish, redirect, delete or materially change live content without an explicit instruction for that change.
- Public forum participation must answer the person’s question first, disclose affiliation where relevant and follow the platform’s rules. No fabricated personas, mass posting, link dropping or duplicate replies.
- Never commit `.env`, credentials, raw analytics exports or personally identifiable data.
- Record publish dates, changed URLs and measurement annotations so later traffic or sales changes can be attributed.
