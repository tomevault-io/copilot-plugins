# anime-adventure-lab

> Tome by [Justin21523](https://github.com/Justin21523/anime-adventure-lab) — distributed by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

An open lab for anime-style interactive stories: LLM story engine, Chinese RAG, SDXL image generation, and LoRA fine-tuning. Upload your own characters & lore, play in the browser, export scenes, and reproduce results with configs, metrics, and Docker Compose
