# medieval-city-builder-starter-kit-threejs

> Tome by [SeloSlav](https://github.com/SeloSlav/medieval-city-builder-starter-kit-threejs) — distributed by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Three.js medieval city builder template: curved dirt roads with PBR blending, bridges, and junctions on procedural hills, forest, and rivers. Place mills, quarries, reforesters, and homes with city-builder camera controls; SpacetimeDB runs the authoritative economy, labor, and road logistics sim.
