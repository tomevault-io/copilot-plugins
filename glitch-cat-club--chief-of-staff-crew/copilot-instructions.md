# The Vinyl Room

An independent record shop's website. The site is in `site/`.

## The shop, as it really is

Use these facts. Never invent a detail about this business.

- The Vinyl Room, 14 Gloucester Road, Bristol, BS7 8AT.
- Open Monday to Saturday. Weekdays 10am to 6pm, Friday until 8pm, Saturday 9am to 8pm. Closed Sunday.
- New pressings, classic reissues, hand graded secondhand. Jazz, soul, post punk, dance.
- Two listening booths at the back, free, first come first served, bookable in advance.
- Special orders take about a week.

## How this page is shown

It is displayed in a **wide, short window, about 1080 by 520**, and **the whole page must fit on that one screen with no scrolling**. Compose it like a gig poster, not a document:

- A **left panel about a third of the width, full height, ink background** carrying the artwork large with the headline plate and strapline stacked under it.
- The **right two thirds** hold everything else as a tight grid of cards: about, hours, the order button. No card floats alone, no dead areas of empty ground.
- Fill the frame edge to edge. Big type, tight gaps (16 to 24px), everything anchored to the grid.
- Body sized to exactly the window: no margins collapsing into empty bands top or bottom.

## House style

**Look.** Neo brutalist and graphic. Flat blocks of colour, no gradients, no photographic softness. Every solid element carries a **4px black border** and an **8px hard offset shadow**, never a blur. Big confident type. Illustration over photography.

Palette, and only this palette:

| | |
|---|---|
| cream `#FFF6DF` | the ground everything sits on |
| ink `#111111` | all borders, shadows and body text |
| pink `#FF2D78` | the loudest accent, used sparingly |
| mint `#9BE7C4` | blocks and panels |
| blue `#4D8DFF` | blocks and panels |
| yellow `#FFD23F` | highlights and plates |

Body text is never an afterthought: **Rubik at weight 500 or heavier, 15px minimum, ink on a light ground**, with real space around it. Thin grey paragraph text is off style.

Headline type is heavy and geometric, ideally Archivo Black or Lexend Mega with a system fallback. Body type is Rubik or a plain sans. Monospace is JetBrains Mono.

Borrow the language of a gig poster: banner ribbons, cream label plates holding black type, bold arcs, a halftone dot texture, a headline where each word takes a different colour from the palette.

**Card anatomy, every card the same skeleton.** Content starts at the top of the card, never vertically centred, never a dead band above the heading. The heading is Archivo Black, small size, letterspaced uppercase, with a 3px ink underline the full width of the card. Below it, body text, then any control. Padding 20 to 24px all round, consistent across every card.

**Ink panels** are never flat black boxes: they carry a 4px cream border and an 8px pink offset shadow, and their text is cream.

**Tables** have an ink header band with cream uppercase letterspaced labels, 3px ink rules between rows, and data figures in the monospace face. No faint hairlines.

**Buttons** are Archivo Black uppercase on pink with the ink border and hard shadow, and press down by their shadow depth on hover.

**Words.** British spelling. **Never use a long dash.** Use a comma, a colon or brackets. No Oxford comma. No Latin abbreviations. Say the thing plainly and stop.
