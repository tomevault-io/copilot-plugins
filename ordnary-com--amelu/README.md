# amelu

> Tome by [ordnary-com](https://github.com/ordnary-com/amelu), graded and kept true across every major AI platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Amelu is an email hosting product by Ordnary. It provisions and manages mailboxes, aliases and domains on top of a self-hosted Stalwart mail server, with its own Postgres database for account and billing metadata, kept separate from Stalwart's own mail store.
