# Contribution guidelines

- Keep each LLM explanation accurate and 10 words or fewer, excluding the term.
- Capture the core mechanism or distinction in plain English, using technical terms when necessary.
- Avoid filler, marketing language, and unnecessary implementation details.
- Match the README's Markdown format, sentence case, and neutral style.
- Verify the word count before finishing.
- Avoid unrelated changes.
