# openfpvcast

> Tome by [1Basco](https://github.com/1Basco/openfpvcast), graded and kept true across every major AI platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

An open-source Android app that casts the video from DJI FPV goggles (N3) to your phone over USB, lets you record the feed (with optional phone-mic audio), change camera settings, and re-broadcast the live view over WebRTC so anyone on the same WiFi can watch in a browser.
