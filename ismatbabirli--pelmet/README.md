# pelmet

> Source: [ismatBabirli/pelmet](https://github.com/ismatBabirli/pelmet). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Reclaim the macOS menu bar icons your MacBook notch hides. Pelmet is a free, open-source, permission-free menu bar organizer: hide clutter and bring it back with a click or ⌥⌘B, with a Shelf that surfaces the icons the notch swallowed. A privacy-first Bartender & Hidden Bar alternative.
