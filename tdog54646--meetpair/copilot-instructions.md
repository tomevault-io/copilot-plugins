## Spec Kit and Superpowers

For complex features, Spec Kit artifacts are the source of truth for requirements, scope, architecture, and tasks.

After spec.md, plan.md, and tasks.md are approved, Superpowers handles implementation discipline: TDD, debugging, review, and verification.

Do not generate a second independent specification or implementation plan. Do not run speckit-implement and a Superpowers execution workflow for the same feature.
