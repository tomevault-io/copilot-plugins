# fosfora

> Source: [kevinraymond/fosfora](https://github.com/kevinraymond/fosfora). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Free, open-source real-time audio & music visualizer / VJ engine, with audio-reactive GPU shaders, particles, and layer compositing, driven by WGSL and controlled with MIDI, OSC, or your phone. Cross-platform (Vulkan/Metal), with NDI, Spout, Syphon, and virtual-camera output.
