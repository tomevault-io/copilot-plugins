> Tome by [Roozbeh-Sdtz](https://github.com/Roozbeh-Sdtz/jarvis-home-commander), graded and kept true across every major AI platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Turn an always-on Mac into a voice-first home commander. "Hey Siri, Call Jarvis" opens a conversational AI agent that controls your whole smart home — HomeKit, Philips Hue, Aqara/Matter, Bosch appliances, energy meters — through local-first APIs. No Homebridge, no cloud lock-in, no port forwarding.
