# AI instruction files for OpenHearing

> Sourced from [HMAKT99/OpenHearing](https://github.com/HMAKT99/OpenHearing) and converted for every major platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Free, open-source hearing assistance for Android — take a hearing check (or enter professional results), build a per-ear sound profile, and amplify speech in real time through any earbuds. Presets, profiles, experimental media EQ. Fully offline: no accounts, ads, or trackers. Not a medical device. Alpha APK on Releases.
