# tilde

> Source: [zhangzhedesign/tilde](https://github.com/zhangzhedesign/tilde). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

a desktop todo tool for Windows. Centered around a compact pinned quick-entry box, it lets you summon it with a single `~` keypress to capture tasks instantly, or hold `Alt` to speak. Tasks are managed in a kanban with pending/in-progress/completed states, and you can export your completed history. Always-on-top is optional.
