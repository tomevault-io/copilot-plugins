## fourierinstrument

> This is for my Fourier Analysis small-scale final project.

This is for my Fourier Analysis small-scale final project.

I want to try implementing a FFT of a musical instrument (i.e. piano), and visualize how things like harmonics and timbre come across when decomposed into frequencies.  Then, I want to try and generalize this by synthesizing different notes informed by the relationship between these frequencies. Then, I want to analyze and interpret any shortcomings this simple method may have.
