> Source: [Stuey3D/VibeSDR](https://github.com/Stuey3D/VibeSDR). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Vibe-coded, but not slop. A mobile-first SDR receiver for iOS, Android & Apple Watch — the live waterfall on your wrist, tuned with the Digital Crown. Multi-backend (UberSDR, OpenWebRX/+, KiwiSDR, FM-DX), on-device USB RTL-SDR, VibeServer, own GPL-free NEON DSP engine. GPLv3, open, credited, checkable.
