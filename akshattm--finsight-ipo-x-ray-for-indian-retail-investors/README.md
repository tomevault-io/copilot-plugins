FinSight is an IPO X-Ray. You open an IPO and instantly get a fact sheet (issue size, fresh issue vs offer for sale, price band, promoters, lead managers, use of proceeds). Every figure is page-cited and clickable. You can then ask questions in English or by Hindi voice. Every number in an answer gets a verified/unverifiable/contradicted badge.
