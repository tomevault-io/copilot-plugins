## Preferences
- On-brand graphics matching the "Better Homes Begin Here" Facebook post style
- Photo-forward with real Cavalier home interiors as backgrounds
- All dates must be verified against a calendar before publishing — see cavalier-social-graphics skill for the 5-step verification process
- Captions should be warm, community-focused, and inclusive of all home journey stages
- No TikTok
- Use the cavalier-social-graphics skill for all social content
- Comment-to-enter over UGC for campaigns under 10k followers
- Jason is dyslexic — proofreading is Claude's responsibility, not Jason's
- Seed engagement (staff, mates, family commenting first) before expecting organic reach
