## release-notes-no-emoji

> User-facing release notes must not use emoji characters


# Release notes style

When creating or editing files under `release-notes/`:

- Do **not** use emoji in titles, `###` section headers, or body copy.
- Follow `release-notes/TEMPLATE.md` and `release-notes/README.md` for structure and tone.
- Prefer clear, scannable headings in plain language (for example, "Navigation improvements" not decorative symbols).
