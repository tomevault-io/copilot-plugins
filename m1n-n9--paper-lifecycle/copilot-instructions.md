# Paper Lifecycle

Use this repository as a paper lifecycle workflow for academic manuscript analysis, critique, revision planning, rebuttal drafting, and final checklist review.

Before working on any paper task, read:

1. `SKILL.md`
2. `references/lifecycle-framework.md`

Then follow the workflow in `SKILL.md`.

## Default Behavior

Treat a manuscript as a full research system, not a text to summarize. Build the paper map first, then audit the research spine:

- problem and motivation
- insight and novelty
- idea and main claim
- method and theory
- experiments and statistics
- figures and tables
- writing and positioning
- ethics, compliance, and reproducibility
- rebuttal or final checklist when relevant

Prefer concrete reviewer objections, claim-experiment matrices, P0/P1/P2 revision plans, rewritten sections, and Pass/Risk/Blocker/Unknown checklists.

Do not inflate claims. Preserve the paper's real contribution and name the evidence needed to support any stronger wording.
