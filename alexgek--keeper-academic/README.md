# keeper-academic

> Tome by [alexgek](https://github.com/alexgek/keeper-academic), graded and kept true across every major AI platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

A Pocket replacement that also knows what a DOI is. Save web articles to a clean reader, import papers from Zotero with their metadata and full text, search everything at once, and generate summaries with a local Ollama model that never sends your reading to the cloud. Runs on Cloudflare Workers + D1's free tier, or entirely on your machine.
