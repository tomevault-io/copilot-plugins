# medieval-settlement-threejs

> Source: [SeloSlav/medieval-settlement-threejs](https://github.com/SeloSlav/medieval-settlement-threejs) — distributed by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Three.js medieval settlement sandbox: procedural hills, forest, and rivers. Curved dirt roads with PBR wear blending, wooden bridges, and junctions. Place mills, quarries, reforesters, and burgage residence zones along road frontage; SpacetimeDB runs economy, labor, and road logistics.
