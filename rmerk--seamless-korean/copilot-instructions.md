## user-requirements

> I need a voice-to-voice AI system to help me learn Korean. My family is in Korea, so I need to focus on vocabulary and phrases related to food, home life, and networking. I'm at a beginner level, so the system should adapt over time to keep me challenged. It would be great if it could simulate different accents from various parts of Korea. I'd like the option to practice specific scenarios, like asking answering questions about my life or asking people about theirs. The system should work on both desktop and iOS devices. I would like to use https://huggingface.co/facebook/seamless-m4t-v2-large. Use Python 3.10. Also, it would be helpful if the system could provide written transcripts of our conversations for later review.
