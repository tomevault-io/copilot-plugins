# AI instruction files for indo-g2p

> Sourced from [snowfluke/indo-g2p](https://github.com/snowfluke/indo-g2p), graded against the public Tome Standard and kept consistent across every major platform by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Indonesian grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) and phonemizer for text-to-speech. Converts Bahasa Indonesia text to IPA phonemes, splits words into syllables, resolves schwa and glottal stops. Zero dependencies, TypeScript, works on Node.js, Bun, Deno, and in the browser.
