# folio

> Source: [woosal1337/folio](https://github.com/woosal1337/folio). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Local-first meeting notes for macOS. Records system audio + mic, transcribes on-device with Whisper, and writes markdown to your vault — your audio never leaves your Mac.
