# youta

> Source: [vitaly-zdanevich/youta](https://github.com/vitaly-zdanevich/youta). Graded, signed and kept current as the models change by [TomeVault](https://tomevault.io)

Low-resource TUI audio player for YouTube (also Music) through yt-dlp, Bandcamp, Apple Podcasts and RSS, with local subscriptions and persistent listening progress, playing through headless mpv. With chapters on the seekbar - from YouTube timecodes. Also local files, Wikidata integration.
